About me

I hold a Master's degree in Cybersecurity from ENSICAEN & the University of Caen. My background covers both offensive security (pentesting, forensics, honeypots) and defensive security (auditing, ISO 27001 governance, SIEM).

Passionate about the intersection of artificial intelligence and cybersecurity, I currently work on the robustness of biometric models against adversarial and backdoor attacks, as well as on detecting these attacks using generative AI.
